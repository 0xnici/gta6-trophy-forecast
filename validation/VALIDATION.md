## What this does and does not show

Both practice histories are a single frozen Exophase page: launch night before the first refresh, every read identical, players tracked constant. On such a series the model's trend shrinks to zero and it forecasts persistence, so the walk-forward margins above measure one thing only — that the model's spread on a quiet series is priced a little tighter than the house's fixed 0.5-per-square-root-day walk, because its floor volatility is 0.35 and the widening terms (residual scatter, step volatility, slope uncertainty, rarity) all read zero or near it on frozen data. The 5–95% band misses 2 of 110 held-out rates across the two cases, and 2 held-out rates are exactly 100%: a quantile row is capped at 99.99, so no band can contain a printed 100%, and that trophy is the miss in each case. None of this says how any entry copes with a refresh of the tracked pool, which is what actually moves the final's rates; the model answers that structurally — trend shrinkage, movement-driven spread, rarity widening — not from anything the practice cases can measure.
